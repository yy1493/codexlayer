1. Who We Are

CodexLayer (“CodexLayer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a mobile game development startup that creates and publishes games and related digital experiences.

This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile games, apps, websites, and services that link to this policy (collectively, the “Services”). This policy covers our games released for Android (including Google Play) and iOS (including the Apple App Store), as well as related services that link to it. A particular game may provide additional privacy information when needed.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the game, the features you use, your device settings, and the third-party services integrated into that game. We may collect the following categories where applicable:

  • Gameplay and app information: game progress, settings, session duration, in-game events, crash reports, and performance diagnostics.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, and approximate region derived from technical signals.
  • Identifiers: app-specific identifiers or advertising identifiers, where available and permitted by your device settings and applicable law.
  • Purchase information: transaction status, product purchased, and related receipt or order references provided by the app store. Payment card details are generally handled by the relevant app store or payment provider, not by us.
  • Information you provide: messages you send to support, feedback, reports, or other information you choose to submit.
  • Information from service providers: limited information supplied by platform, analytics, advertising, anti-fraud, or crash-reporting providers that support a game.

3. How We Use Information

Where applicable, we use information to:

  • operate, maintain, and provide our games and Services;
  • save gameplay progress and remember in-game preferences;
  • process or validate purchases and respond to support requests;
  • diagnose crashes, detect abuse or fraud, protect service integrity, and improve performance;
  • understand aggregated gameplay trends and improve game design;
  • show ads or measure advertising performance, where a game uses advertising;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We use information only for purposes that are appropriate to the context in which it was collected and as permitted by applicable law.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Depending on the game and applicable law, we may disclose limited information to:

  • Service providers that help host, maintain, analyze, secure, or support our Services;
  • Advertising and measurement partners if ads or ad measurement are enabled in a game;
  • App stores and platform providers to support distribution, purchases, platform features, and compliance;
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Some privacy laws treat certain ad-related disclosures as “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or similar activities even when no money changes hands. Where applicable, we will honor required choices and opt-out rights.

Third-party services used in our games: We use Firebase Analytics and the advertising networks listed in Section 5. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. The specific SDKs and features active in a particular game may vary.

We use the advertising networks and mediation services listed below in our Android and iOS games. These providers may process device and app information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from technical information, and information about ad delivery or interactions, depending on the SDK, configuration, device permissions, and applicable law. The providers may use information to deliver, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, subject to their policies and your available privacy choices.

Depending on your location and device, you may be able to limit advertising personalization through your device settings or a consent interface presented in the game. These controls may not stop all ads; they may affect how ads are selected.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the Services, maintain game progress, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by information type and service.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can also uninstall a game to stop its collection of information through that installation. Uninstalling may not delete information already held by us, an app store, or a third-party provider; contact us if you want to request deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended to collect personal information from children in a way prohibited by applicable law. The age at which a person is considered a child varies by location, and additional requirements may apply to games directed to children or mixed audiences.

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us. We will review the request and take appropriate steps as required by law.

9. International Data Transfers

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data protection laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.
