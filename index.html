1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games, websites, and related services operated by CodexLayer (“CodexLayer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) that link to this policy.

Individual games may have additional privacy notices or features. If a game provides a separate notice, read that notice as well.

2. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the game, the features you use, your device settings, and the third-party services integrated into that game. Depending on those factors, information may include:

  • Gameplay and diagnostics: game progress, session duration, settings, crash reports, performance data, and interaction events.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system, app version, language, time zone, IP address, and identifiers used by the operating system or service providers.
  • Purchase information: transaction status, product purchased, and related purchase records provided by the app store. Payments are generally processed by the relevant app store; we do not receive your full payment card details through that process.
  • Information you provide: messages sent to support, feedback, or details you choose to submit through an in-game feature.
  • Account or online features: if a game offers accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, multiplayer, or social features, information needed to operate those features may be processed.
  • Advertising and attribution data: where applicable, ad interactions, advertising identifiers, and campaign attribution information may be processed by us or our advertising partners.

We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for the relevant game or service. Do not submit sensitive personal information through support or feedback features.

3. How we use information

Where applicable, we use information to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain our games and features;
  • save progress and support gameplay, purchases, leaderboards, or multiplayer functionality;
  • identify bugs, prevent fraud or abuse, and improve performance and player experience;
  • respond to support requests and communicate service-related information;
  • measure game performance and, where enabled, advertising effectiveness;
  • comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

Where required by law, we rely on an appropriate legal basis for processing, such as providing a requested service, legitimate interests, consent, or compliance with a legal obligation.

4. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may disclose relevant information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: providers that help us host services, analyze crashes or usage, provide customer support, process purchases, or deliver other game features.
  • Advertising partners: if a game includes advertising, ad partners may process certain device, identifier, or interaction data under their own privacy terms.
  • Other players: information you choose to make public through leaderboards, profiles, multiplayer, or social features may be visible to other users.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, or defend our rights.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, investment, restructuring, or transfer of some or all of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Review those policies where relevant.
